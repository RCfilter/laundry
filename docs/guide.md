# My Laundry System

Why this setup uses **365 Unscented Powder**, **Dirty Labs Bio Enzyme Laundry Booster**, and **food-grade citric acid** (dissolved in water for the softener drawer) — and deliberately avoids **liquid detergent** and **fabric softener** on a new machine.

---

## The three-product stack

| Role | Product | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Main detergent | **365 Unscented Powder** | Surfactants, enzymes, oxygen bleach — mud, dinginess, general cleaning |
| Enzyme boost | **Dirty Labs Bio Enzyme Booster** | Extra lipase/protease for body oils & gym wear — replaces liquid “sport” detergent |
| Rinse / feel | **Food-grade citric acid** (dissolved in water) | pH balance, rinse aid — **not** fabric softener |

**Not used:** liquid detergent, liquid fabric softener, softening dryer sheets.

---

## Why no liquid detergent or fabric softener?

Goal: avoid **physical and chemical “hooks”** — residue in the machine or on fabric that future soil, oils, or bacteria can attach to.

### Fabric softener — avoid (strong agreement)

Liquid softener leaves a **waxy hydrophobic coating** that traps body oils, hurts towel absorbency, and can film HE drawers/gaskets. **Citric acid in the rinse** improves feel by removing detergent/mineral film — different chemistry, no persistent wax layer.

### Liquid detergent — reasonable protective choice (with nuance)

| Real concern | How this stack addresses it |
|---|---|
| Drawer gunk from viscous liquids | Powder (+ booster) in **drum**; no liquid cup buildup |
| Hard-water curds | Powder builders + citric acid rinse |
| HE overdose residue | Measured doses + **extra rinse** |
| “Need liquid for body oils” | **Dirty Labs** lipase instead of liquid sport |

**Nuance:** Many people use HE liquid successfully. Problems usually come from formula, dose, and water — not “liquid” alone. This approach trades liquid convenience for lower buildup risk on a **new** machine. That’s valid.

**“Hooks”** = undissolved granules, sticky films, soap scum (detergent + minerals), fatty residues, biofilm growing on any of the above.

## What each product does

**365 Powder:** surfactants, lipase, oxygen bleach, builders. ~½ scoop per normal load; drum before clothes.

**Dirty Labs Booster:** concentrated enzymes (lipase for sebum). Follow label. Cumulative effect on gym odor.

**Citric acid:** 1–2 tsp dissolved in room-temp water (acid into water) → softener drawer. Not a softener.

---

## How to run a load

1. Powder + booster in drum before clothes  
2. Warm/hot when safe  
3. Pre-dissolved citric acid in softener drawer  
4. Extra rinse  
5. Prompt removal; door/drawer open to dry  

| Load | Powder | Dirty Labs | Citric acid | Temp |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Everyday | ½ scoop | Per label | 1–2 tsp dissolved | Warm |
| Whites/towels/bedding | ½ scoop | Per label | 1–2 tsp | Hot |
| Gym | ½ scoop | Don’t skip | 1–2 tsp | Warm |
| Delicates | Reduce | Skip/minimal | 1–2 tsp | Cool |

## Gym clothes & odors

Polyester traps oils → butyric acid smell when warmed. This stack helps via lipase, no softener, citric rinse, extra rinse. Still: wash soon after use, don’t overdose, clean gasket, expect gradual improvement.

---

## Protecting a new machine

Wipe gasket; open door/drawer; monthly tub clean + drawer wash; weekly seal disinfect; periodic pump filter; annual dryer vent. Jar-test any new product first.

### Jar test (quick)

Cup of tap water + ½ tsp detergent → shake foamy → wait. Scum/curds that **won’t redissolve** = buildup risk with your water.

---

## FAQ

**Must I avoid all liquid?** Not scientifically required — sensible risk reduction for a new HE machine. **No fabric softener** is the harder rule.

**Why dissolve citric acid?** Even dispensing; no crystals in drawer.

**Powder + booster overkill?** Overlap exists; booster most valuable for gym/heavy soil.

**Pre-treat stains?** Small amount of liquid **on the fabric** (not through the drawer) is a common exception.
